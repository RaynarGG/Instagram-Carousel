PSYCHOLOGY.

THE EMPTY REASON WORKED

AS WELL AS THE REAL ONE

No reason at all: 60% stepped aside. A real reason: 94%. A reason that meant nothing: 93%. It didn't have to be a reason — it only had to have the shape of one.

THEN THEY ASKED FOR 20 PAGES

INSTEAD OF FIVE

No reason: 24%. Empty reason: 24% — not one person more. Only a real reason still moved anyone, and only to 42%. The trick dies the moment something is at stake.
