PSYCHOLOGY.

A WHEEL OF FORTUNE

STOPS ON A NUMBER

People watch a wheel of fortune stop on a number. Then they are asked: what percentage of United Nations members are African countries? Should the wheel matter?

PSYCHOLOGY.

WHEEL SAID 10: GUESS 25

WHEEL SAID 65: GUESS 45

Median answers: 25% after the wheel showed 10, 45% after it showed 65. The wheel had nothing to do with Africa. Money for accurate answers did not make the pull go away.

PSYCHOLOGY.

WE START AT A NUMBER

AND STOP TOO SOON

The authors call it anchoring: people begin at a starting value and adjust from it. The adjustments are typically insufficient, so the answer stays close to where it began.

SAME PRODUCT. 5 SECONDS.

TWO ANSWERS.

High school students estimated 8×7×6×5×4×3×2×1 or 1×2×3×4×5×6×7×8. Median guesses: 2,250 when it started high, 512 when it started low. The correct answer: 40,320.
