(https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/). Bitstream Vera derivative, permissively
licensed for embedding/redistribution — see the project's license page.
Used here only for burned-in reel-clip subtitles; the account's brand font
(Anton) is not used because it exists only as .woff2, which libass/ffmpeg
cannot load directly.
